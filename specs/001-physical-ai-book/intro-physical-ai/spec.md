## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Understand Core Concepts (Priority: P1)

As a student, I want to read the introduction chapter so that I can understand the core concepts of Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence.

**Why this priority**: This is the foundational knowledge required before proceeding to subsequent technical chapters.

**Independent Test**: A student can define Physical AI and explain embodied intelligence principles after reading the chapter.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** I have read the "Introduction to Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence" chapter, **When** asked to define Physical AI, **Then** I can provide an accurate definition.
2.  **Given** I have read the "Introduction to Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence" chapter, **When** asked to explain embodied intelligence, **Then** I can describe its principles and significance.

### Edge Cases

- What happens if a reader has no prior AI or robotics knowledge? The chapter should provide clear, accessible definitions and context.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The chapter MUST define Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence clearly and concisely.
- **FR-002**: The chapter MUST discuss the convergence of AI and robotics, including human-robot interaction and ethical considerations.
- **FR-003**: The chapter MUST cover the foundational concepts, historical context, and future trends relevant to Physical AI.
- **FR-004**: The chapter MUST adhere to academic clarity and readability standards (Flesch-Kincaid grade 9-12).
- **FR-005**: The chapter MUST not contain any plagiarized content.

### Key Entities

- **Physical AI**: The concept of artificial intelligence implemented in physical forms.
- **Embodied Intelligence**: AI that learns and interacts with the world through a physical body.
- **Humanoid Robotics**: Robots designed to resemble and interact with humans.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: The chapter provides accurate and comprehensive definitions for Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence.
- **SC-002**: The chapter effectively communicates the historical progression, current state, and future directions of Physical AI and robotics.
- **SC-003**: The chapter successfully meets all content quality constraints specified in the main project constitution (e.g., no plagiarism, academic clarity).
